Jewellery purchase & specification certificate

Your certificate records the purchase date, client and item description, metal type and weight, number of stones and total carat weight. Its stone schedule records shape, carat, colour, clarity and certification numbers where applicable, alongside purchase price and authentication.

This seller-issued record of sale is separate from an independent diamond grading report. It is not an independent valuation. For insurance, probate or open-market valuation, obtain a report from a qualified independent valuer.
