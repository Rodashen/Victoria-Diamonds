Diamond stud earrings: is carat weight per ear or per pair?

By Victoria Diamonds · 29 October 2026 · 3 min read · English

Does a pair of 1 ct diamond studs mean 1 ct in each ear?

Not necessarily. If 1 ct is the total weight of a matched pair, each equal diamond weighs 0.50 ct. If each earring contains a 1 ct diamond, the pair totals 2 ct. Always check whether the description refers to one stone, one earring or the complete pair, including any smaller accent stones.

Cluster earrings require a different calculation. Add every diamond in one earring, then account for the second earring. A larger number of stones is not evidence that the centre diamond itself has the stated total weight.

Can you judge stud size from carat alone?

Carat describes weight, while the outline and dimensions describe how much space a diamond occupies. The basket, halo or bezel can also change the apparent size of the earring. Compare measurements and the complete setting, rather than using a close-up image as a scale drawing.

Decide whether you want a discreet everyday pair or a more noticeable look. Bring a photograph of earrings you already enjoy wearing to a consultation and explain what you would like to change.

What should you ask about the complete pair?

  • Is the price for one earring or two?
  • What is the individual stone weight and the total pair weight?
  • Does the total include accents or a halo?
  • What metal, post and backing are included?
  • What grading information and aftercare accompany the pair?

Ask about the backing and overall construction as well as the front view. Comfort is a practical part of the decision, especially for a pair you expect to wear often.

How should you check a Victoria Diamonds earring order?

Read the original design details and the supported configuration on the product page. Some earring designs have fixed diamond groups and counts, even when metal choices remain available. Do not assume every pair uses one solitaire diamond on each side.

Before payment, confirm that the named product, selected materials, complete price and pair specification agree with your intention. Daily Sparkle includes stud and earring designs; contact the atelier if the distinction between per-stone and per-pair information is unclear.

Common questions

What is the total for two 0.50 ct diamonds?

The pair totals 1.00 ct, provided there are no additional diamonds to include.

Is the largest-looking stud always the heaviest diamond?

No. Shape, measurements and the surrounding setting affect appearance. Check the actual stone specification.

Can I change the diamond count on every earring?

No. Some designs have fixed counts and sizes. Use the options offered for the piece or contact the atelier about a different brief.

A CLOSER LOOK

Check whether the weight is per stone or per pair

A pair described as 1 ct total could contain two approximately 0.50 ct stones. Ask whether the stated weight covers the complete pair and whether the backs are included.
