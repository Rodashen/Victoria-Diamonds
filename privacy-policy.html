United Kingdom Privacy Policy

Victoria Diamonds respects your privacy and handles personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We only collect personal information when it is necessary to provide our services, respond to enquiries, arrange appointments, process custom jewellery requests, or communicate with you about our collections.

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, order or enquiry details, communication history, and any information required to process a bespoke design or appointment request. We process this data for the purposes of customer service, order fulfilment, appointment scheduling, marketing communications where you have given consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

Your personal data may be shared only with trusted service providers, including CRM and email systems, payment processors where required, and professional advisers who provide legal, accounting, or operational support. We do not sell personal data and we do not keep it for longer than necessary for the stated purpose.

You may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data, and you may withdraw your consent to direct marketing at any time. Please contact us at info@victoriadiamonds.com or via the contact details on our website if you would like to exercise these rights.

We use cookies and similar technologies for browsing, security, and analytics where consent has been provided. Where legally required, we will obtain your consent before activation of non-essential cookies.

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

香港私隱政策

維多利亞鑽石重視您的私隱，並按照《個人資料（私隱）條例》（香港法例第486章）及相關要求處理個人資料。當需要提供珠寶服務、回覆查詢、安排預約、處理定制珠寶要求或與您聯繫時，我們會收集必要的個人資料。

我們可能會收集您的姓名、電郵地址、電話號碼、訂單或查詢資料、溝通紀錄，以及處理私人訂制或預約時所需的相關資料。我們處理此資料的目的包括客戶服務、預約安排、訂單處理、行銷通訊（在您已明確同意的情況下）及遵守法律責任。

我們僅會將個人資料提供予受信賴的服務供應商，例如 CRM 系統、電子郵件平台、必要的付款處理服務及法律/會計顧問。我們不會出售個人資料，且只會在達到目的所需的期間內保留資料。

如您希望查閱、修正、刪除或限制處理個人資料，或撤回市場推廣的同意，您可透過官方聯絡渠道與我們聯絡。我們將按香港法例要求處理您的申請。

我們會在必要時使用 Cookie 及類似技術，以維持網站安全、提供功能及改善體驗；如於香港網站上選擇繁體中文（香港）語言版本，我們會依照香港法定要求處理相關同意及私隱安排。

如您對我們的個人資料處理方式有疑問，或認為內容不合適，您可根據香港相關法例向持牌監管或有關機構提出投訴。
