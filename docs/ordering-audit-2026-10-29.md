## Image-review limitations and outstanding production details

All 88 ring images and their available catalogue configurations were reviewed. These images are design visualisations, not manufacturing drawings. Stones hidden behind a band, under a setting, or obscured by perspective cannot be reliably counted. A visible count cannot establish carat weight. No universal ten-stone or 0.10 ct default was invented.

Examples requiring an authoritative stone schedule before further count changes:

- Solenne, Splendor Stacking, Venus, Athena, Fae, Skylar, Celestia, Elysia, Lunelle, Stellar Promise, Eternal Harmony and Elysian show pavé/accents but their presets only contain a centre stone.
- Endless Devotion and Ethereal Halo have a recorded four-stone accent preset, but images show additional pavé/halo stones. Exact full counts and any distinct halo size require production confirmation; their counts were not changed arbitrarily to ten.
- Destiny Couple, Eternal Connection and stacking sets need a stone schedule for each component; combining a visible front count across separate rings would be misleading.
- Élégance Unie's catalogue says two 0.50 ct stones and 5 ct total, while the preset is one 1 ct centre. These records conflict and cannot establish the missing accents.
- Curved Chevron, Marquise & Round Alternating, Amour, Lumière Eternity, The Knot, Eternal Promise and Amour Éternel require full-band schedules; a front image does not reveal the full count.
- Aura pavé/halo styles and several three-stone designs show small accents absent from centre-only or centre/side presets. Retained recorded sizes until a complete schedule is supplied.

These are open data-quality findings, not certified matches between all photographs and production specifications. Existing quote controls still show the priced configuration and its total.

## Natural Tennis rules

Only shape and carat per stone are customer-controlled. H–K has central colour grades I/J; VS2 is the central clarity between SI1 and VS1; cut is Excellent. Select the actual priced I/J, VS2, Excellent entry nearest their midpoint price. Send its catalogue ID and automatic whole-stone quantity to the existing calculator; do not invent an averaged client price.

Stone count uses the catalogue's total carat weight (12 ct for most designs, 21.75 ct from 29 × 0.75 ct for Apex/Pillar). Sizes that cannot form that total with whole stones are unavailable rather than silently rounding or changing the design total. Original Lab budgets and bracelet tiers remain unchanged.
