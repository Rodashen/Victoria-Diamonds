Our Heritage

Three Generations of Golden Mastery

Established in the vibrant heart of Hong Kong, Victoria Diamonds draws its inspiration from the city's centuries-old legacy as the crossroads of the East. Deeply rooted in the ancient traditions of Chinese metallurgy and the historic maritime trade routes that once brought the world's finest gems to the Pearl of the Orient, our atelier began as a humble goldsmithing workshop. Today, our master artisans preserve these time-honored, dynastic techniques, ensuring that every piece crafted carries the weight of history and the enduring elegance of our heritage.

Today, we bring this art in London to continue the tradition, blending time-honored techniques with contemporary design sensibilities. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the weight of this legacy.

Jenny Pun

CEO and Founder

The Art of Creation

Crafted by Hand, Treasured Forever

Each Victoria Diamonds piece undergoes a meticulous journey from concept to completion. Our artisans employ traditional goldsmithing techniques passed down through generations, ensuring every curve, texture, and finish meets our exacting standards.

  • Hand-selected materials from ethical sources
  • Traditional lost-wax casting and hand-forging
  • Multiple quality inspections at every stage
  • Personalized finishing and custom sizing
