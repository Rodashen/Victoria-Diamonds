Gifting guide

Matching jewellery for pet lovers: what should you choose?

By Victoria Diamonds · 29 October 2026 · 3 min read · English

What is a thoughtful personalised gift for a pet lover?

Choose a motif connected to their cat or dog, then decide whether the gift is for the owner, the pet or a matching pair of pieces. Victoria Diamonds’ Pet Lover Collection includes Sterling 925 diamond jewellery for Furmom and personalised decorative charms for Furbaby. Each piece is priced and sold separately.

What can you choose for Furmom?

The For Furmom selection includes pendants, rings and bracelet charms with cat- and dog-inspired motifs. Start with a type of jewellery the recipient already wears. A pendant can suit someone who enjoys necklaces; a ring needs a reliable size; a bracelet charm needs an appropriate bracelet or attachment arrangement.

Pendants and bracelet charms are sold without chains. Check what the recipient already owns and ask the atelier about dimensions or compatibility before assuming that a separate chain will fit.

What is included in a Furbaby charm?

The For Furbaby selection contains decorative cat and dog collar charms. The designs include a fixed 0.10 ct lab-grown diamond and Sterling 925 silver. Collars and separate hooks shown in the wearing demonstrations are not included.

The illustrations help explain the styling; they do not establish the fit of the charm on every animal or collar. Check the dimensions and attachment arrangement with the atelier. A decorative charm should not be assumed to replace an identification tag or another required accessory.

What should you check before personalising?

Furbaby order forms ask for a pet name of up to eight characters. Type the name exactly as you want it recorded and check spelling before submission. The names visible in the product images are examples, not the name that will automatically be supplied.

Optional reverse engraving allows up to 40 characters, subject to available space. Keep the request concise and ask about layout if it is important. The two Furmom rings also need a UK ring size; our ring-size guide explains what to confirm before ordering.

How do you order matching pieces?

Each product page links to its matching design. Open both pages and compare the motif, recipient and listed diamond specification. Choose the pieces you want individually: a matching photograph does not mean both items are included in one listed price.

Prices currently listed in the collection range from £65 to £165 per piece, with fixed silver and diamond specifications and full payment online. The product page gives the applicable price for that design. Keep the confirmation for each order and contact the atelier if you need help coordinating a gift.

Common questions

Is the matching Furmom piece included with a pet charm?

No. Each piece is sold separately. Follow the matching-piece link to view and order the companion design.

Are a collar, hook or chain included?

Collars and separate hooks are excluded from the pet charms. Pendants and bracelet charms exclude chains. Check the exact product description.

Can I change the diamond size on these Silver pieces?

The silver and diamond specifications are fixed. Use the personalisation fields offered, or contact the atelier about a different request.
