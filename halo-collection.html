A complete stone schedule

Each design has one oval centre and ten hidden-halo diamonds. Band accents vary by design. Halo and band stones are 0.05 ct round diamonds; their counts and sizes stay fixed while you choose your centre carat and preferred metal. Lab starts at 2 ct; the Natural reference starts at 1 ct and requires an appointment for complete accent pricing. All starting prices use Essential metal pricing and 18K yellow gold. Protected shipping is free on orders over £1,000.

Images show the original 1 ct design concepts. Final stone placement, dimensions and fit are confirmed during CAD and sampling.
