Our Heritage

Generations of Golden Mastery

Established in the vibrant heart of Hong Kong, Victoria Diamonds draws its inspiration from the city's centuries-old legacy as the crossroads of the East. Deeply rooted in the ancient traditions of Chinese metallurgy and the historic maritime trade routes that once brought the world's finest gems to the Pearl of the Orient, our atelier began as a humble goldsmithing workshop. Today, our master artisans preserve these time-honored, dynastic techniques, ensuring that every piece crafted carries the weight of history and the enduring elegance of our heritage.

Today, we bring this art in London to continue the tradition, blending time-honored techniques with contemporary design sensibilities. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the weight of this legacy.

Jenny Pun

CEO and Founder

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our bespoke jewelry services

Do you offer custom ring designs?

Yes! You can work directly with our design team to create a bespoke setting, choose your center stone (natural or lab-grown), and approve a 3D preview before crafting begins.

How does the custom design process work step-by-step?
  1. Initial Consultation: We discuss your ideas, preferred metals, gemstone choices, and budget.
  2. Design & 2D/3D Rendering: We share detailed visual concepts or 3D CAD renders for your review.
  3. Stone Selection: You choose your exact center diamond or gemstone.
  4. Crafting: Once design CADs and stone selections are approved, our master jewelers craft your piece.
How long does a custom order take?

Custom pieces typically take 2 to 4 weeks from final design approval to delivery.

Can I supply my own gemstone or heirloom piece to re-set?

Yes. We can incorporate family heirloom stones into new custom settings, subject to a physical inspection by our jeweler to verify the stone's structural integrity before setting.

Do you offer lab-grown or natural diamonds?

We offer both certified ethically sourced natural diamonds and high-grade lab-grown diamonds, allowing you to choose the option that aligns best with your values and budget.

What is the difference between lab-grown and natural diamonds?

Chemically, physically, and optically, lab-grown and natural diamonds are identical—both are pure crystallized carbon. Natural diamonds are formed over billions of years deep within the Earth, whereas lab-grown diamonds are created in controlled laboratory environments. We offer both so you can choose the stone that best aligns with your preferences and budget.

Are your diamonds certified?

Yes. All center stones above 1 carat come with an independent grading certificate from accredited gemological laboratories such as GIA or IGI.

How much is the deposit for a custom piece?

We require a 50% deposit of the total estimated cost upon approval of the initial quote and design direction. The remaining 50% balance is due upon final completion, prior to dispatch or collection.

Can I transfer my deposit to a different design?

If you decide to modify your design concept before manufacturing begins, your deposit can be applied toward the new design. However, additional costs may apply if new 3D CAD renders or different gemstones are required.

Can I request a custom engraving?

Complimentary text engraving is available on most standard wedding bands and ring shanks (up to 20 characters), depending on the band width and setting design.

What if I order the wrong ring size?

We offer one complimentary ring resizing within 30 days of delivery for most standard band designs.

What is your return policy?

In-stock jewelry can be returned or exchanged within 30 days in its original, unworn condition. Custom or personalized items are final sale.

Do you offer a manufacturing warranty?

Yes. Every Victoria Diamonds piece comes with a lifetime manufacturing warranty covering structural defects in craftsmanship. It does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental loss, or unauthorized third-party alterations.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we provide fully insured express international shipping to select destinations worldwide via specialized high-value carriers (such as FedEx Express or DHL Express).

Transparency

Our Diamond Sourcing & Pricing Transparency

At Victoria Diamonds, we believe transparency is the foundation of true luxury. We want you to know exactly what you are investing in and how we source the finest lab-grown diamonds directly for your bespoke pieces.

To maintain uncompromising standards, we work exclusively with high-quality stones, categorized into two carefully curated tiers to help you understand the value behind each selection.

Luxe

Color: D

Clarity: IF / F

Ideal For: The top end of polish, rarity, and light performance.

Select

Color: E / F / G

Clarity: VS+

Ideal For: Exceptional brilliance and striking value with beautiful near-colorless character.

How We Source For You

Direct Selection: We bypass traditional retail markups by hand-picking your stone directly from certified, sustainable lab-grown diamond creators.

Strict Grading: Every diamond must meet our strict requirements for cut, polish, and symmetry to ensure maximum light performance.

Custom Matching: Whether it is a 0.1 ct accent for our new silver collection or a major centre stone, each diamond is meticulously matched to your design specifications.
