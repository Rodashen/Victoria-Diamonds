United Kingdom Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies on our website to keep the site functioning correctly, protect against fraud and abuse, maintain security, and improve the browsing experience. Some cookies are strictly necessary, while others are optional and only activated if you consent.

Essential cookies are required for site navigation, security, and form performance. These cookies allow pages to load correctly and help maintain secure visits.

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve content and functionality. These are optional and require consent under UK law.

Marketing cookies may be used to support newsletters, campaigns, and personalised offers, where appropriate and where consent has been obtained.

You can accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or set your preferences at any time. Your choices can also be updated by revisiting this page or using the cookie control in the banner available on the website.
