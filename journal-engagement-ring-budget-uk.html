Buying guide

How much should you spend on an engagement ring in the UK?

By Victoria Diamonds · 29 October 2026 · 3 min read · English

What is a sensible engagement ring budget?

A sensible budget is an amount you can comfortably spend on the complete ring. There is no need to follow a months-of-salary rule. Decide which features matter most, then compare written quotes for the same diamond origin, stone weights, setting and metal. A loose-diamond price alone is not a finished-ring price.

Which parts of the ring should you prioritise?

Choose three priorities before comparing prices: perhaps a particular diamond outline, a low-profile setting and yellow gold. Separate these from preferences you could change. This makes a conversation about alternatives more useful than asking for the largest possible stone at a single price.

Think about how the ring will be worn. A design that looks appealing in a close-up image still needs the right fit, setting height and proportions for its wearer. Share practical preferences with the designer alongside the visual brief.

How can you compare engagement ring quotes fairly?

Make a small comparison sheet. Record whether the diamond is natural or lab-grown, the centre-stone weight, any side stones, metal and purity, and what is included in the finished setting. GIA’s engagement-ring buying questions provide a useful framework for discussing preferences with a jeweller.

Two rings with the same headline carat weight may have different stone counts or settings. Check whether a price is a starting configuration, a selected complete quote or only the amount due as a deposit.

What can you change if a design exceeds your budget?

Ask for alternatives one at a time: a different stone weight, a simpler setting, another available metal specification or a different diamond origin. Keep a note of what changes between each proposal, so you can see whether the saving affects something important to you.

A natural-versus-lab-grown comparison should explain origin as well as price. Avoid treating either choice as a promised investment return. Choose the combination you want to own and wear.

What should be confirmed before you pay?

  • The total ring price in GBP and the exact selected specification.
  • The deposit, remaining balance and when that balance is due.
  • Any sizing, engraving or delivery costs relevant to your order.
  • What grading documentation accompanies the selected diamond.
  • Agreed production timing and the applicable order terms.

On Victoria Diamonds product pages, a supported online configuration is checked by the payment server before ordering. For a new bespoke brief, review the commission checklist with the atelier.

Common questions

Does a starting price include every possible upgrade?

No. A starting price refers to a particular configuration. Changing available materials or diamond selections can change the complete quote.

Is a deposit the full cost of a ring?

No. A deposit is part-payment. Check the complete order total and remaining balance before proceeding.

Can I discuss a fixed budget with a designer?

Yes. Give a realistic range and explain your priorities. The team can discuss which available choices fit the brief.
