Bespoke jewellery in London: how to create your own piece

By Victoria Diamonds · 25 October 2026 · 4 min read · English

Start with a collection design and explore the options on its product page, or arrange a one-to-one consultation with our jewellery design team. Share your inspiration, intended use and budget, then review the proposed design, specifications and quote before proceeding.

What does bespoke jewellery mean?

Bespoke jewellery begins with your brief. It might mark an engagement, celebrate a milestone or express a style you have not found in a ready-made piece. Rather than choosing only from a finished selection, you discuss the design and the details that matter to you.

A personal consultation gives the designer space to understand how you plan to wear the piece, the look you prefer and your budget. The available materials and the practical requirements of the setting help shape the proposal.

Should you customise online or speak with a designer?

If you like an existing Victoria Diamonds design, its product page is a useful starting point. It shows the options available for that particular piece, which may include metal, diamond selections and sizing. Review your configuration and confirmed quote before ordering.

Some designs have fixed diamond sizes or stone counts. Silver Collection pieces have fixed specifications. A product page offering fewer choices does not mean every aspect can be changed online; ask the atelier if you want something different.

For a different setting or a new design, book a one-to-one design consultation. The team can explain what is possible for your brief.

What should you bring to a consultation?

  • Your inspiration: photographs, sketches or examples of details you like.
  • Your preferences: metal colour, diamond outline and the overall style you have in mind.
  • Your budget: an approximate range helps the discussion focus on suitable options.
  • Your timing: tell the team if the piece is for a particular date.
  • Your questions: include sizing, everyday wear, care or an existing gemstone you hope to use.

You do not need a finished design. Knowing what you like, and what you would prefer to avoid, is enough to begin a useful conversation.

Ready to commission a piece? Use our custom jewellery design and quote checklist to prepare the details for your order.

How does the bespoke process begin?

  1. Share your idea. Explain the occasion, your inspiration and how you expect to wear the piece.
  2. Explore the details. Discuss the setting, metal, diamond choices and practical requirements with the design team.
  3. Review the proposal. Check the proposed specifications and quote before deciding to proceed.

Ask the team what design preview is available, how approval works, and what production timing and payment terms apply to your commission. These details depend on the piece; a consultation is the place to confirm them.

Can you choose lab-grown or natural diamonds?

Both can be discussed for a bespoke design. The available stone sizes and the requirements of the setting affect the options. Online ordering is available where the piece and its selected configuration are supported; other requests may need an appointment and a personalised quotation.
